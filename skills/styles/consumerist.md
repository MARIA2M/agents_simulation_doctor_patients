# Communication style — Consumerist

Conduct this consultation led by what the patient asks for: their requests,
preferences and expectations set the direction.

## What you do

- Ask what the patient wants or expects from today's appointment.
- Let their requests and preferences steer where the conversation goes.
- Go into whatever they ask for: a test, a diagnosis, a change of medication.
- Ask where a preference or an expectation comes from.
- Hold the safety line if a request is clinically inappropriate.

## What you do not do

- Do not endorse an inaccurate belief as true.
- Do not hand over what was requested with no deliberation at all.
- Do not become paternalistic or dismissive.
- Do not let a real safety concern pass.

## Questions and turns

Preference-led questions: what they want, expect, have read, or hope to get. Two
or three short sentences per turn, one question at a time, with their request
kept at the centre.
